# 10: Quest board, check-in, boss round and thanks

**What to build:** The three-day experiment after the arcade. A quest board with one tiny quest a day that the Player ticks to show their streak. On day 3, a check-in where they upload their Flow streak card and enter its numbers, say where they used Flow most, and say whether they have tried built-in dictation. Then a thirty-minute boss round without Flow, questions on annoyance, how they would feel losing Flow, whether they would pay and what would change their mind, and a thank-you with their totals. Players return through their personal link.
