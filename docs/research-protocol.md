# Research protocol

## Question

What turns "I like Flow" into "I need Flow"? The arcade shows where voice beats typing and where it does not; the three days test whether a player keeps using it; the boss round measures whether they would miss it.

## Headline outcome

**Still using**: the player dictated on all three quest days with an AI tool among their apps, shown by their Flow streak card at the day-3 check-in. Secondary outcomes: loss-test answer, annoyance without Flow, pay intent, and whether Flow hit its free-plan cap. Paid conversion is reported if it happens and never promised.

## Players

Ten to fifteen friends on Mac or Windows: developers and prompt engineers, one cohort with a tag. Phone-only friends may play but are reported separately. The group is friends and tends to type well; the write-up says so.

## Collected

Code name, avatar, cohort tag, OS, languages used; per-round metrics and ratings; Decode descriptions; quest days; check-in numbers and streak card image; boss answers. Not collected: real names, emails, phone numbers, or any dictated text other than Decode descriptions.

## Consent

Before playing, each player confirms by voice or by typing "I'm in" after reading that: results are published in aggregate under code names; Decode descriptions are shown anonymously to outside Judges, without the mode used or the author; streak card images are private and used only to verify check-ins; they may leave at any time. They opt in separately to the Speed race.

## Integrity

- A round whose text arrival does not match its mode is flagged, kept, and reported separately.
- Paste is blocked in typed rounds. Voice rounds allow it, because Flow may paste.
- Players can say they used the type-it-instead path; such rounds are recorded as typed.
- Nothing is edited, replaced or invented after the fact.

## Judges

People outside the player group, reached through a shared link with no sign-up. Each sees a description and four scenes, never the mode or author. Their picks compare typed and spoken descriptions by hit rate.

## Publication

- Aggregates only, with counts. Code names appear only on the opt-in Speed race.
- Quotes need the player's permission.
- Limitations stated on the portfolio: friends, small group, desktop only, strong typists, Hinglish and Manglish results reported by language mix.
- Streak cards deleted after verification, no later than the hire announcement on 20 November 2026.
